Questions

A few things people ask before trying it.

How does the analyzer decide two clauses are related?

It compares the key terms in each sentence, ignoring common words. Sentences that share enough of them are treated as covering the same topic.

What kinds of conflicts does it find?

Different numbers with the same unit (days, hours, percent, money), opposite permissions such as "must not" versus "may," and a required rule that another document treats as optional.

Is it a replacement for legal review?

No. It is a fast first pass that points reviewers to the clauses worth reading closely. Wording and context still need a person's judgment.

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. This page analyzes text locally in your browser.
